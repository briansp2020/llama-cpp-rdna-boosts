# M3 — the stream-K per-row partial contract: chunked staging costs ~1.5 % per chunk beyond two

**2026-10-27.** Build `217b1c999` (r158 tip) + `PROTOTYPE-2-streamk-partial.patch` (wip only, never in
`patches/`), ROCm 10.1.0, gfx1201 (R9700), `HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES` pinned, every run sequential. This is
`TODO` #114's step 0 plus the M2 matrix re-run on the working protocol.

## 0. What changed against M2

M2's blocker was that the stream-K partials live in two contracts (a tile-start block publishes a
*final normalized* value, a seam block an unnormalized partial + meta, and the fixup only visits tiles
with a seam), so a tile whose KV sits inside one block publishes no `(max, rowsum)` at all — which is
what produced the NaNs.  The fix is exactly the handover's §5:

* **`kv_partial_meta`**, a new trailing pointer on `fattn_kernel_t` (the tile/vec kernels take it
  unnamed and ignore it).  Non-null = the per-row PARTIAL contract: a **no-seam** tile skips the
  normalize and publishes its own `(max, rowsum)` into the element-major buffer (with `np > 1` the
  combined `KQ_cmn` is carried in the free 3rd float of `tile_Q`'s per-column padding, because the
  combine block is the only place that has it); the two stream-K fixup kernels, in the same mode, hand
  back the merged but still **unnormalized** value + its `(max, rowsum)` instead of dividing.
* **`kv_start`**, one new trailing int: the chunk's first KV row in the whole op's cell space.  The mask
  and `cell_pos` pointers are offset per chunk (so the kernel's tile indexing is chunk-local), while the
  two *absolute* tables stay whole-op and are sliced exactly — `KV_max` is a multiple of
  `FATTN_KQ_STRIDE` and chunks are `lcm(nbatch_fa, FATTN_KQ_STRIDE)`-aligned, so
  `(kV_max - kv_start)/nbatch_fa` and `(kv_start + kb0*nbatch_fa)/FATTN_KQ_STRIDE` are exact.
* **Launcher**: `GGML_CUDA_FA_STAGE_CHUNK=<rows>` (default off).  One ring slot (`chunk_slots = 1`, not
  the prototype's 2 — the conversion of chunk `c+1` and the launch of chunk `c` are enqueued in that
  order on the main stream, so the slot cannot be overwritten under a live launch); ping-pong
  accumulator (chunk 0's buffer **is** the accumulator), online merge, one finalize.
* Chunking only engages with **≥ 2 chunks** — one chunk means the ring is the prefix anyway.

## 1. Step 0 — the row contract alone (no chunk boundaries)

4B Q8_0, q8_0 KV, `-c 4096 --chunks 4`, `GGML_CUDA_FA_STAGE_CHUNK=100000` (clamped to one chunk):

| arm | PPL |
|---|---|
| off (shipping) | **7.4661** |
| one chunk, partial contract + fixup + finalize | **7.4661** |

Identical to 4 decimals, as the handover required: only the publish/merge order of an existing split
changed, and moving the single divide from the fixup into the finalize is bit-exact.

## 2. M2 matrix re-run — the mechanism now costs ~1.5 % per chunk, not 2.8x

Qwen3.8-27B UD-Q4_K_XL (16.34 GiB), **1 GPU**, `-fa on -ctk/-ctv q8_0`, `-ub 512 -b 2048`,
`llama-bench -p 8192,16384,32768 -n 0 -r 3`.  Ring = 1 slot, both operands (2 KiB/row on this model).

| arm | ring (flat) | pp8192 | pp16384 | pp32768 | Δ at 32k |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| off (whole-prefix arena) | 134 MiB @32k | **1315.01** | **1245.08** | **1139.41** | — |
| `CHUNK=16384` | **64 MiB** | 1307.79 | 1246.99 | **1139.56** | **+0.0 %** |
| `CHUNK=8192` | 32 MiB | 1307.67 | 1245.31 | 1106.85 | −2.9 % |
| `CHUNK=4096` | 16 MiB | 1305.53 | 1223.84 | 1089.72 | −4.4 % |
| `CHUNK=2048` | 8 MiB | 1292.05 | 1208.28 | 1069.59 | −6.1 % |

(The `off` arm reproduces M2's recorded 1313.89 / 1243.65 / 1140.08, i.e. the protocol change is inert
when chunking is off.)

Depth curve, same model, `-p 65536 -r 2`: off **971.68**; `CHUNK=32768` (2 chunks) **970.98** (−0.1 %);
`CHUNK=16384` (4 chunks) **931.90** (−4.1 %).  `CHUNK=12288` at pp32768 (3 chunks) **1121.46** (−1.5 %).

**The cost is a function of the chunk count, not of the chunk size**: 2 chunks are free at 32k and at
64k, then ~1.5 % per extra chunk, flattening to ~6 % by 16 chunks.

### Where the time goes (pp32768, `CHUNK=8192`, diagnostic skips — wrong results, timing only)

| arm | pp32768 |
|---|---:|
| chunked, as shipped | 1105.61 |
| + skip the merge kernel | 1107.38 (+0.2 %) |
| + skip the fold kernel too | 1108.17 (+0.2 %) |
| + skip the per-chunk conversion too | 1113.72 (**still −2.2 % vs off**) |

So the merge, the fold and the per-chunk conversion are worth ~0.2-0.4 % each; **the remaining ~2.2 % is
inside the FA kernel itself** when its KV extent is short (the stream-K work split is
`blockIdx.x*total/gridDim.x` over `iter_k*ntiles_dst`, and a chunk's `iter_k` is smaller).  That is the
open optimisation, and it is what stands between 4+ chunks and parity.

## 3. Arena — the whole point

27B, q8_0 KV, 33 024-token prompt, `llama-cli -v`:

```
off:               FA_STAGE request=1.0 MiB → 17.0 MiB → alloc=16.0 → 68.0 → 160.0 MiB
CHUNK=16384:       FA_CHUNK n_kv=33024 rows=16384 chunks=3 ring=64.0 MiB     (flat)
```

At `-c 262144` the whole-prefix request is the reporter's **1024 MiB** (512 MiB/operand cap × 2); the
ring stays **64 MiB** at any depth (16x smaller there, and it no longer participates in the r156 growth
ladder / issue #138 stranding at all).

## 5. Bugs this session found and fixed (all were invisible to a 27B-only run)

1. **Attention sinks were counted once per chunk.**  A sink contributes `exp(sink - max)` to the ROWSUM,
   so `n_chunks` partials each carried it (149 op-test cases failed, all `sinks=1`).  Fix: only chunk 0
   gets `sinks`; the merge then treats the sink as one more `(max, rowsum)` pair, which is exact.
2. **The no-seam publish was gated on the wrong thing.**  It hung off the `stride_k == warp_size` pass of
   the DV loop, whose k range is **empty when `nbatch_combine < warp_size`** (16 < 32 on the head-512/576
   and head-320 tiles) — so those tiles' no-seam rows published nothing and came out ±inf (7 op-test
   cases).  Fix: gate on `k == 0`, which the first non-empty pass always covers.  The 27B (nbatch_combine
   64) never showed it, which is why the campaign measurement would not have caught it.
3. **The packed fully-masked-group bitmap cannot be sliced by a chunk start** (its per-tile word count is
   derived in-kernel from the launched KV length), so a chunked launch disables it.  The *derived*
   (batch-wide) layout — what a single-sequence prefill uses — slices exactly via `kv_start`.  Threading
   the whole-op word count is the follow-up; on this box the skip is worth ~0 at prefill
   (`GGML_CUDA_FA_MASK_SKIP=0` at 32k: 1143.57 vs 1140.08, M2).
4. **A copy is not free.**  The first cut copied chunk 0 into the accumulator and only chunked at ≥2
   chunks; that copy alone cost 2.1 % at pp8192 (1 chunk everywhere).  Ping-pong + "no chunking for one
   chunk" removed it.

## 6. What this means for the campaign decision (README §0)

Outcome **A (tile it) is reachable** — the mechanism works, and the arena is O(chunk) — but it is a
**knob with a measurable trade**, not a free win:

* `CHUNK=16384` (ring **64 MiB flat**): prefill **+0.0 %** at 32k, −4.1 % at 64k, KLD 0.001 at 2 chunks,
  greedy text unchanged.  At `-c 262144` it bounds the reporter's 1024 MiB arena by 16x.
* `CHUNK=2048-4096` (8-16 MiB): −3 to −6 % prefill at 32k and the prefill-logit gate at/over its limit.

The unresolved half is the ~2.2 % that lives in the FA kernel's *short-KV* execution (§2); closing it is
what would make the small-chunk arms free, and nothing measured here says it is impossible.  The
maintainer's decision, to put explicitly: **default-on with a bound (and −4 % at 64k), or a knob**, and
whether the follow-up is worth a campaign of its own before anything is promoted.
