# 2026-10-30 — PR #252 (`wip/graph-order-r176`) on r176: three decode graph orderings, two shipped

Contributor `@briansp2020`, filed as `wip/graph-order-r176/` against r176, applying with `git am` unchanged.
Assessed on gfx1201 (3x R9700, 1 GPU and 2-GPU `-sm tensor`) and gfx1100 (7900 XTX, `fingon`), same source
path for every arm (`~/llama.cpp`, `build-pr252` vs the r176 `build-rocm`/`build-r176`), `-DAMDGPU_TARGETS`
matching each box, `ROCBLAS_USE_HIPBLASLT=0` on every fresh-process comparison, `-t 8`, alternating rounds.

## The three patches

| patch | what | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 0001 | `graph_optimize` moves a Q-norm attention layer's V / K projections in front of its Q `MUL_MAT` (`GGML_CUDA_QK_ROTATE`) | **taken** |
| 0002 | the dense 1-token row concat also runs on RDNA4 (`GGML_CUDA_MMVQ_ROW_CONCAT_RDNA4`) | **taken** |
| 0003 | gather small GDN projections (`ssm_beta`, `ssm_alpha`, `attn_gate`) into the row concat (`GGML_CUDA_MMVQ_CONCAT_REORDER`) | **held off** (`TODO` #119) |

0001 is the mechanism: with V/K/Q adjacent, the row concat takes all three GEMVs (not just V+K) and
`ggml_cuda_qk_pair_k_at`'s overlap test stops refusing the Q/K norm+rope pair (the allocator had been handing
V/K the Q chain's freed buffers).  Same nodes, one stream, structural match only — so bit-identical, and the
measurements below confirm it.

## 0003 could not be exercised

The pass needs a **quantized** `MUL_MAT` with `<= 256` rows and skips `Q8_0`.  Tensor types in the models we
hold:

```
Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q4_K_M  blk.0.ssm_alpha.weight  F32  [2048, 32]
                           blk.0.ssm_beta.weight   F32  [2048, 32]
                           blk.0.attn_gate.weight  Q8_0 [2048, 4096]
Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q3_K_M / UD-Q5_K_M / Q6_K / Q8_K_XL   ssm_alpha, ssm_beta: F32
Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-Q8_0                                      ssm_alpha, ssm_beta: Q8_0 (skipped)
```

A scan of every GGUF on both boxes finds one `<=256`-row quantized weight anywhere (the DFlash2 drafter's
`selector_hidden`, Q4_K).  Measured on the 35B with the pass on vs off: **gfx1201 104.4 / 104.9 vs
105.1 / 105.2 t/s**, **gfx1100 141.1 / 141.1 vs 141.1 / 141.1** — neutral to −0.7 %.  Its claimed +3.0 %
(RDNA4) / +3.5 % (RDNA3) must come from a quant whose GDN small projections stay quantized, i.e. a standard
(non-UD) k-quant we do not have.

## Notes for a future edit

* The rotate lives in the **alloc-deps** half of `ggml_backend_cuda_graph_optimize`, so the meta backend's
  `marks_only` forward sees the unrotated order while `allocs_only` sees the rotated one.  That is fine for
  every matcher we have (marks are per tensor), but a new adjacency-dependent matcher in the marking half
  would have to account for it.  The 2-GPU `-sm tensor` text + logit identity above is the check.
* V/K stay live across the Q chain, so the compute buffer grows a little (512 floats each per layer at
  decode).  No measured VRAM or `--fit` consequence on any arm here.
* The RDNA4 row-concat gate is "every segment has an even row count", because the 1-token RDNA4 launch may
  take two rows per block and a 2-row block must not straddle a segment boundary.  It rests on
  `calc_nwarps_weight` being row-independent at `ncols_dst == 1` — re-check that before widening it.
